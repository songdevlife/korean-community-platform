## 3. Meaning of Personal Information

Personal information is information or an opinion about an identified individual, or an individual who is reasonably identifiable, whether or not the information is true or recorded in a material form.

Sensitive information is a category of personal information that may include information about a person's:

-   racial or ethnic origin;

-   political opinions;

-   religious beliefs;

-   sexual orientation;

-   health;

-   disability;

-   biometric information; or

-   criminal record.

DAK does not ordinarily require users to provide sensitive information. Users should avoid posting unnecessary sensitive information in public areas of the platform.

Some of the services DAK relies on store or process personal information outside Australia. The providers currently in use, and the countries involved, are:

## 20. Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for:

-   Providing DAK services

-   Maintaining account and community integrity

-   Security and fraud prevention

-   Resolving disputes

-   Enforcing policies

-   Maintaining financial and transaction records

-   Complying with legal obligations

DAK does not currently operate automatic deletion after a fixed period. Information is retained while it is needed for the purposes above, and is deleted when you ask us to delete it or when we determine it is no longer required.

In practice this means:

-   Account information: retained while the account exists, and deleted when you close your account or ask us to delete it

-   Content you have published: remains visible until you delete it or ask us to remove it

-   Search records and anonymous website analytics are retained until manually removed as part of DAK's data management practices. DAK does not currently apply automatic deletion periods to analytics records, and they are not linked to any account or identifiable individual.

-   Payment and taxation records: retained as required by applicable law

-   Backup copies: retained for disaster recovery only, and overwritten in the ordinary course of backup rotation

If you want your personal information deleted, contact us using the details in section 29 and we will action the request unless we are required or authorised by law to retain it.

We will introduce scheduled deletion as the platform develops, and this section will be updated to describe it before it takes effect.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to destroy it or de-identify it, unless retention is required or authorised by law.

Deletion from active systems may not immediately remove information from secure backups.

Secure backup copies will be retained only for disaster recovery and security purposes and will be overwritten or deleted in accordance with DAK's backup retention practices.
