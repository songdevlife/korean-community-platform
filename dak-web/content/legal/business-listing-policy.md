# Business Listing Policy

**Effective Date:** **[TO BE COMPLETED: EFFECTIVE DATE]**

## 1. Purpose

This Business Listing Policy explains the rules for creating, managing, and maintaining business listings on Discover Adelaide Korea ("DAK").

Our goal is to provide accurate, trustworthy, and useful information for our community while ensuring fair treatment of all businesses.

## 2. Eligibility

Businesses may be listed on DAK if they:

-   Operate legally in Australia or provide services relevant to the
Adelaide Korean community.

-   Provide accurate and truthful information.

-   Comply with all applicable laws and regulations.

DAK may refuse or remove listings that do not meet these requirements.

## 3. Listing Information

Business owners are responsible for ensuring that all listing information is accurate and up to date.

This includes, where applicable:

-   Business name

-   Description

-   Contact details

-   Website

-   Social media links

-   Address

-   Opening hours

-   Photos

-   Services offered

-   Pricing information (if provided)

Providing false or misleading information may result in suspension or removal of the listing.

## 4. Verification

DAK may verify a business through methods such as:

-   Email verification

-   Phone verification

-   Business website verification

-   Australian Business Number (ABN) verification

-   Other reasonable verification methods

Verification improves trust but does not guarantee the quality of a business or its services.

## 5. Ownership Claims

If multiple users claim ownership of the same listing, DAK may request evidence of ownership before granting management access.

Examples of evidence include:

-   Business email domain

-   ABN details

-   Official documentation

-   Other reasonable proof of authority

## 6. User Reviews

Businesses may not:

-   Create fake reviews.

-   Ask others to submit misleading reviews.

-   Offer incentives in exchange for undisclosed positive reviews.

Businesses may respond to reviews respectfully but must not harass or threaten reviewers.

## 7. DAK Exclusive Promotions

Businesses participating in DAK Exclusive promotions must:

-   Clearly describe the offer.

-   Honour the published terms during the promotion period.

-   Avoid misleading or deceptive advertising.

DAK reserves the right to remove promotions that are inaccurate, expired, or misleading.

## 8. Listing Updates

Business owners should promptly update their listings when information changes, including:

-   Contact information

-   Opening hours

-   Location

-   Services

-   Temporary closures

-   Permanent closures

Repeated failure to maintain accurate information may affect listing visibility.

## 9. Prohibited Content

Business listings must not include:

-   False claims

-   Illegal products or services

-   Copyright-infringing material

-   Offensive or discriminatory content

-   Spam or keyword stuffing

-   Malware or harmful links

## 10. Suspension or Removal

DAK may suspend or remove listings that:

-   Contain misleading information.

-   Repeatedly violate this Policy.

-   Receive credible reports of fraudulent activity.

-   Threaten user safety.

-   Breach applicable laws.

Where appropriate, DAK may request corrections before removing a listing.

## 11. Search Ranking

DAK may consider various factors when displaying business listings, including:

-   Relevance to the user's search.

-   Listing completeness.

-   Verification status.

-   Recent updates.

-   User engagement.

-   Reviews and ratings.

DAK does not guarantee any specific ranking or placement in search results.

Paid features, if introduced, will be clearly identified and will not alter the accuracy or integrity of search results.

## 12. Intellectual Property

Business owners must have the right to use all photos, logos, trademarks, and other materials submitted to DAK.

By uploading content, the business grants DAK a non-exclusive licence to display and use that content for operating and promoting the platform.

## 13. Disclaimer

DAK provides business listings for informational purposes only.

Inclusion on DAK does not constitute an endorsement or guarantee of a business, its products, or its services.

Users are responsible for making their own decisions before engaging with any listed business.

## 14. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Business Listing Policy from time to time.

Material changes will be published on the DAK website.

Continued use of DAK constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

## 15. Contact

Questions regarding this Policy may be submitted through DAK's official support channels once the service launches.
