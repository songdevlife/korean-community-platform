# Accessibility Statement

**Effective Date:** 31 July 2026

## 1. Our Commitment

Discover Adelaide Korea ("DAK") is committed to providing a website and services that are accessible and inclusive to as many people as reasonably possible.

We strive to ensure that users of all abilities can access our platform and participate in our community.

## 2. Accessibility Goals

We aim to:

-   Design clear and consistent user interfaces.

-   Support keyboard navigation where practical.

-   Provide sufficient colour contrast.

-   Use readable fonts and layouts.

-   Include descriptive alternative text for important images where
appropriate.

-   Support responsive design across desktop, tablet, and mobile
devices.

-   Continuously improve accessibility as the platform evolves.

## 3. Standards

Where practical, DAK aims to align with the principles of the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA**.

As our platform grows, we will continue improving accessibility features and addressing identified issues.

## 4. Third-Party Content

Some third-party content, embedded services, maps, videos, or external websites linked from DAK may not fully meet our accessibility standards.

We encourage users to contact the relevant third-party provider where accessibility issues relate solely to their services.

## 5. Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing process.

We regularly review user feedback and consider accessibility improvements when developing new features or updating existing functionality.

## 6. Feedback

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of DAK or have suggestions for improving accessibility, please contact us through our official support channels admin@discoveradelaidekorea.au.

If you identify an accessibility issue, please provide the page URL, device, browser, and a description of the issue so we can investigate it.

We will make reasonable efforts to address verified accessibility concerns.

## 7. Changes to this Statement

This Accessibility Statement may be updated from time to time.

The latest version will always be available on the DAK website.

*Last Updated: 31 July 2026*
