# Community Guidelines

**Effective Date:** **[TO BE COMPLETED: EFFECTIVE DATE]**

## 1. Our Community

DAK exists to help Korean-speaking residents, students, visitors, and anyone interested in Korean-related services in Adelaide connect, share reliable information, and support one another.

We encourage respectful discussions, helpful contributions, and a welcoming environment for everyone.

## 2. Be Respectful

Treat other users with respect.

Do not:

-   Harass or intimidate others.

-   Use abusive or offensive language.

-   Personally attack individuals.

-   Encourage discrimination or hate.

-   Threaten violence.

Healthy disagreement is welcome. Personal attacks are not.

## 3. Post Accurate Information

Before posting information:

-   Check that it is accurate.

-   Clearly distinguish facts from personal opinions.

-   Avoid spreading rumours or misinformation.

When sharing important public information, include the original source whenever possible.

## 4. Spam and Advertising

Do not:

-   Post repetitive content.

-   Flood discussions.

-   Send unsolicited promotional messages.

-   Create multiple accounts to promote content.

-   Manipulate engagement.

Business promotion should use the appropriate business listing or approved promotional features where available.

## 5. Reviews

Reviews should:

-   Reflect genuine personal experiences.

-   Be honest and fair.

-   Focus on the service or experience.

Do not:

-   Publish fake reviews.

-   Accept payment for positive reviews without disclosure.

-   Coordinate review manipulation.

## 6. Illegal or Harmful Content

The following content is prohibited:

-   Illegal activities.

-   Fraud or scams.

-   Identity theft.

-   Copyright infringement.

-   Malware or phishing.

-   Dangerous or unlawful instructions.

## 7. Offensive Content

Do not post content containing:

-   Hate speech.

-   Racist remarks.

-   Sexual exploitation.

-   Graphic violence.

-   Extreme harassment.

-   Content that promotes illegal activity.

## 8. Privacy

Respect everyone's privacy.

Do not publish another person's:

-   Phone number

-   Home address

-   Email address

-   Identity documents

-   Financial information

-   Personal photographs without permission

## 9. AI-Generated Content

AI-generated content is permitted provided that:

-   It is not misleading.

-   It does not impersonate others.

-   It complies with these Guidelines.

-   The user remains responsible for its accuracy.

## 10. Business Listings

Business owners should:

-   Keep information accurate.

-   Update opening hours.

-   Respond professionally to customers.

-   Avoid misleading claims.

Repeated false information may result in listing suspension.

## 11. Australia Updates

DAK may publish AI-assisted summaries of publicly available information.

Users should not repost copyrighted material in full.

Whenever possible, link to the original source instead of copying substantial portions of the content.

## 12. Reporting Content

Users may report content they believe violates these Guidelines.

Reports are reviewed by DAK moderators.

Submitting false or abusive reports may itself violate these Guidelines.

## 13. Enforcement

Depending on the severity of the violation, DAK may:

-   Remove content.

-   Issue a warning.

-   Temporarily restrict features.

-   Suspend an account.

-   Permanently ban an account.

-   Remove business listings.

Serious or repeated violations may result in immediate account termination.

## 14. Appeals

If you believe moderation action was taken in error, you may contact DAK through our official support channels.

Appeals will be reviewed in good faith, but moderation decisions remain at DAK's discretion.

## 15. Changes

These Community Guidelines may be updated from time to time.

Continued use of DAK after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised Guidelines.
